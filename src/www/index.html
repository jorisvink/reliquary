Secure tunnels between your devices, for hackers, by hackers.

Introduction

The Reliquary is a shrouded sanctum cathedral infrastructure which helps you establish PQ-secure P2P E2EE secure links between any of your devices, even when behind NAT.

The Reliquary provides an API which interacts with our cathedrals and allows you to easily manage your groups of devices (called flocks) using a handful of very simple shell scripts.

Any sanctum based application will work with this cathedral network, be it sanctum itself or libkyrka based applications such as: tier6, confessions and gospel

Follow our guide to get your first two devices connected to each other.

Who runs Reliquary?

This community service is run by the core developers of sanctum with the help of a few volunteers from across the globe. The Reliquary was started so that they could more easily setup tunnels between all their devices without having to use services like Tailscale or Zerotier.

Reliquary is free, please read our terms and conditions. If you prefer self-hosting, the entire reliquary source is available on our github mirror here.

Do you have access to my traffic?

No.

The cathedrals cannot inject, modify or read any of your traffic that is relayed over them because they lack the keys to be able to do so, you own those keys.
